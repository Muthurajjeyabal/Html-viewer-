Privacy Policy

HTML Viewer — Last updated: August 2026

In short: HTML Viewer does not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal data or files to any server. Everything you open stays on your device.

What this app does

HTML Viewer lets you open, view, and edit HTML files and folders stored on your own device. All processing — reading files, rendering pages, editing code — happens locally, inside your browser or device. Nothing you open is uploaded anywhere.

Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any personal identifiers.
  • We do not upload the files or folders you open to any server.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not sell or share any data, because we don't collect any.

What is stored, and where

The app stores a small amount of data only on your own device, using your browser's local storage (IndexedDB). This is never sent to us or anyone else:

  • Recent files list — file/folder names and, where your browser supports it, a reference that lets the app reopen them later.
  • Bookmarks — pages you've chosen to pin for quick access.
  • Version history — snapshots of files you've edited and saved within the app, so you can undo changes.
  • Preferences — settings like zoom level, night mode, and auto-reload, stored via your browser's local storage.

You can clear all of this at any time by clearing the app's storage/data from your device or browser settings.

Permissions the app may use

  • File/folder access — only when you explicitly choose a file or folder via the system file picker. The app cannot access any file you haven't selected.
  • Clipboard — only when you tap "Copy Page Path," to copy text to your clipboard.
  • Screen capture — only when you tap "Screenshot," and only for that single action.
  • Camera — this app does not use your camera. QR codes are generated on-screen for others to scan with their own camera; the app does not scan anything itself.

Third-party services

Two optional features load small, well-known open-source code libraries from a public content delivery network (cdnjs.cloudflare.com) the first time they're used:

  • A code-editor library (for syntax highlighting in the file editor)
  • A QR-code generator library (for the QR Code Share feature)

These are static code files, not tracking services — they don't receive any of your file content or personal data. Loading them requires an internet connection the first time; after that, your browser caches them for offline use. If you never use the editor or QR features, these libraries are never loaded.

Children's privacy

This app does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.
